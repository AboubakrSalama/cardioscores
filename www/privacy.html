CardioScores — Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

CardioScores performs all calculations locally on your device.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, patient data, or usage analytics.
  • No accounts. There is no sign-up and no server component.
  • Local storage. The only value saved on the device is your acknowledgment of the professional-use disclaimer.
  • External links. Some scores link to third-party official calculators (e.g., STS, GRACE); their sites have their own privacy policies.

Questions: contact the developer.

Medical disclaimer

CardioScores is an educational, clinical decision-support reference for healthcare professionals. It is not a medical device, does not provide diagnosis or treatment, and does not replace clinical judgment, guidelines, or local protocols. Verify all results against primary sources before clinical use.
