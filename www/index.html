Before you continue

CardioScores is an educational, clinical decision-support tool intended for use by qualified healthcare professionals.

  • Scores are calculated from published algorithms, but transcription or implementation errors are possible. Always verify against the primary reference before clinical use.
  • A few complex or proprietary models (e.g., STS PROM, STS/ACC TVT, ACS NSQIP, SYNTAX II, Seattle HFM, MEESSI) are simplified local reproductions or approximations — each says so in its own notes. Use the official tool for reporting.
  • No score replaces clinical judgment, guidelines, or local protocols.
  • No patient data is transmitted or stored; all calculations run on your device.
